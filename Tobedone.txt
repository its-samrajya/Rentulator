17. For the receipt.ts
    Here's the actual scenario I'm worried about:

   1. Today, John pays rent for Room 5. A Payment record gets created.
   2. Someone opens the receipt page for that payment. It shows "Room 5" — correct.
   3. Three months later, John moves out of Room 5 into Room 8.
   4. Someone reopens that same old receipt link (maybe John kept it, or an admin is looking back at payment history).
   5. The receipt page runs getReceipt again — it doesn't re-show "Room 5" anymore. It shows Room 8, because occupancies: { where: { moveOutDate: null } } always fetches whatever room the tenant is currently in, not whatever room they were in when that payment happened.
   6. So the receipt for a 3-month-old payment now silently shows the wrong room — one the tenant wasn't even living in when they made that payment. That's misleading for something that's supposed to be a fixed record of what happened.
